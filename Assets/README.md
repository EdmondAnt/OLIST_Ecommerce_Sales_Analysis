## 1. Business Background
### 1.1 Client Background
Olist is a Brazilian ecommerce platform, that offers comprehensive set of online retail solutions. Some of their areas of business include ecommerce marketplace, ERP system, marketplace integration, credit and financial solutions, POS system and shipments.

For this analysis, I will use a data on Olist ecommerce platform operations between an Septermber 2016 and October 2018. As a financial year I will use a period between 2017/08 and 2018/08.

Olist Book of Business consisted of 96k unique customers and more than 3k sellers that spans almost all Brazil. Annual revenue between August 2017 and 2018 reached 12.4 million brazilian reals, steadely increasing YoY.

One of the largest drivers of the growth was Health and Beaty category, which expanded by more than 215%, from almost 300k Brazilian Reals between 2016/10 and 2017/10 to 935k between 2017/10 and 2018/10 and shows a big increase in number of orders. So understanding it success factors and improving it will move whole business.

This report is prepared for the Category management teams and Head of Operations, since it evaluates annual performance for the last complete year. This comprehensive review provides insights on sales trend, customer segmentation and satisfaction, logistics performance, sellers growth and key drivers of performance in these domains.
